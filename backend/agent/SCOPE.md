# Agent scope

This chatbot is a staff productivity tool. It answers telesales, IT, and leadership. The shop keeps talking to a person.

Accepted work: help staff look up stock, Web price, piutang, order status, the app catalog, and knowledge, including extra customer fields when those fields are provided. The staff member still writes the sales order. 

Declined work: a bot that talks to the B2B customer. That includes a shop login on chat, a WhatsApp number, an outbound message, a follow-up sender, and any tool whose receiver is the shop. The claim that this makes 30 telesales people do the work of 100 is a headcount cut. Do not build that.

A birthday field, a fuller customer record, or a data lakehouse does not cross the line. A channel from this agent to the shop does. If that channel is ordered later, decline that piece. It is a separate product, and it is not this task.
